# genAI_policies
This repository contains a selection of institutional and publishing policies on the use of generative AI in academic research. The university sample comprises 65 Italian universities and special-status higher education institutions, identified according to the institutional framework of the Italian Ministry of University and Research, and the top 30 universities in the Times Higher Education World University Rankings, selected on the basis of the research quality indicator as of 17 September 2026. The journal sample included 168 international legal journals associated with 18 publishers selected for their relevance to the research group's areas of interest, alongside 20 Italian journals covering private law, financial law, law and technology, biolaw, comparative law, and family law. 

Both corpora were constructed from publicly available primary sources collected between June and September 2026. For universities, the analysis encompassed AI policies, institutional guidelines, ethical codes, strategic plans, and other relevant documents. For journals, the sources included author guidelines, publication ethics statements, submission instructions, peer-review policies, publication agreements, and publisher-level AI frameworks. Institutions and journals for which no relevant AI policy could be identified were also retained in the sample, since the absence of explicit regulation constitutes a relevant empirical finding.
